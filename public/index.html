How to play
  1. Everyone gets the same secret Telugu movie — except the imposter. Everyone also sees a list of 20 possible movies (the real one plus 19 look-alikes with the same heroes, directors or genres).
  2. Go around and each say one clue about the movie without naming it. Too obvious and the imposter catches on; too vague and you look suspicious.
  3. The host opens voting. Vote for who you think is the imposter.
  4. If the imposter is caught, they get one chance to guess the movie from the 20 and steal the win.
  5. Crew win: +1 each. Imposter win: +2.
